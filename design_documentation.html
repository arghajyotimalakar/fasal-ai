1. Design Strategy for Rural Farmers in Bharat

Over 73% of Indian farmers are non-English speakers. Traditional agri-apps fail because they require multi-step logins, complex terminology, and high-bandwidth downloads. Fasal AI is engineered with radical simplicity:

  • Zero-Friction Camera Access: No login barriers or passwords. The camera viewfinder opens instantly.
  • Voice Narration in Hindi (🔊): Semi-literate farmers can tap to listen to diagnostic results and treatment instructions read aloud in clear Hindi.
  • Dual Treatment Model: Provides both certified chemical treatments and zero-cost indigenous organic remedies (neem, buttermilk, turmeric).
  • Direct National Helpline: Prominent one-tap integration with the government Kisan Call Centre (1800-180-1551).
