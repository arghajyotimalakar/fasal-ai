Problem – Proposed Solution

Core problem / Proposed Solution (Describe your Concept / Idea / Solution / Prototype)
• Detailed explanation of solution
Fasal AI is a mobile-first Progressive Web App. A farmer takes a photo of their diseased crop → Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision AI identifies the disease in 30 seconds → The farmer receives instant treatment advice in Hindi with voice output, including chemical and organic remedies — completely free.
• How it addresses the problem
✅ Eliminates 40–80 km travel to reach an agronomist.
✅ Detects disease before 50%+ crop loss occurs.
✅ Hindi + voice output removes literacy/language barrier.
✅ No app download: works in any browser via PWA.
✅ Works in Demo Mode even without internet.
• Innovation & uniqueness
🤖 First app using Gemini 2.5 Flash multimodal vision for crop disease in Hindi.
🌿 Dual treatment: chemical + organic home-remedy alternatives.
🔊 Hindi voice output reads advice aloud for low-literacy farmers.
⚡ Demo Mode: works offline — no dependency on API key for hackathon demo.
📊 Ground Reality vs. Fasal AI Solution
❌ Today in Rural India
• 30–50% yield lost to undetected diseases annually
• Only 1 agronomist per 1,000 farmers
• Farmers wait 3–7 days for diagnosis — by then it's too late
• 73% of farmers can't use English-only apps
✅ With Fasal AI
• Disease detected in 30 seconds from photo
• Diagnosis in Hindi with voice output
• Chemical + organic home remedies provided
• One-tap Kisan Call Centre helpline: 1800-180-1551
• Zero cost, zero app download required

Feasibility and Risk Analysis

• Analysis of the Feasibility of the Solution
✅ Zero infrastructure cost: Next.js serverless — runs on free Vercel tier or Government NIC cloud.
✅ Works on basic Android phones: PWA under 1MB. Any browser from 2020+.
✅ Google Gemini API is free up to 15 requests/minute on the free tier — sufficient for pilot deployment.
✅ Demo mode: App is fully functional for demos without any API key — reduces deployment friction.
✅ KVK / CSC deployment ready: Can be installed as a PWA on tablets at Common Service Centres.
• Potential Challenges & Risks
1. Low network speed: 2G/3G in remote areas may slow image upload.
2. Camera quality: Blurry photos from low-end phones may reduce AI accuracy.
3. Language diversity: India has 22 official languages — Hindi alone is not sufficient.
4. Digital literacy: Very elderly farmers may struggle with smartphone use.
5. AI hallucinations: AI may occasionally misidentify rare diseases.
• Strategies for Overcoming Challenges
1. Image compression: Auto-compress before upload; offline queue for poor connectivity.
2. Photo guidance UI: On-screen framing guide + brightness tips before capture.
3. Multi-language roadmap: Expand to 8 regional languages in Phase 2 using i18n.
4. AgriWorker model: ASHA-style Village Agri Workers use on behalf of farmers.
5. Confidence scores: Show AI confidence %; escalate low-confidence cases to KCC helpline.

Team Composition
• Roles
🧑‍💻 Full-Stack Developer (Lead): Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, API routes, Gemini AI integration, PWA setup, and deployment pipeline.

🤖 AI & Prompt Engineer: Gemini 2.5 Flash prompt design, structured output schema for bilingual crop disease data, fallback mode logic.

🌾 Agricultural Domain Expert: Crop disease knowledge base, Hindi advisory content, treatment validation with ICAR references, KVK consultation.

🎨 UI/UX Designer: Farmer-first mobile interface design, Hindi typography, WCAG accessibility, voice UX, low-literacy color system.
• Challenges Overcome
🔧 Bilingual AI Responses: Designing prompts that force Gemini to return structured JSON with BOTH English and Hindi (Devanagari) content simultaneously.

🎙️ Hindi Voice Output: Implementing Hindi TTS using Web Speech API without any paid service.

📱 Rural UX Design: Designing for farmers with low smartphone experience — large tap targets, minimal text, visual-first, dark green colors (recognizable as nature/farming).

⚡ Demo Mode: Building a zero-dependency fallback that lets judges see the full UI experience without needing a live API key during presentation.
• What the Team Would Like to Share About Their Journey
Our inspiration came from a news report about a Punjab wheat farmer who lost ₹2 lakh in one season because Yellow Rust was identified too late — after 60% of his crop was already destroyed. He had no smartphone, and the nearest KVK agronomist was a 3-hour bus journey away. We asked: what if he could take a photo and get an answer in 30 seconds in Hindi? That became Fasal AI. We built the entire prototype in 72 hours, testing it on real crop photos from open agricultural datasets. Every design decision was made asking: "Would a 55-year-old farmer in rural UP understand this?"
